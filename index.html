Privacy Policy for Roam.Do

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Roam.Do is a travel checklist app. It is designed to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Summary

Roam.Do does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app requests no Android runtime permissions and has no network access.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter stays in private storage on your device:

  • Your lists and items — titles, notes, links, locations, completion state and ordering — in a local database.
  • Photos you attach — taken with the camera or chosen through the system photo picker, then copied, resized, into the app's own private folder. The app cannot browse your photo library; it only receives the individual images you select or capture.
  • Your preferences — theme and colour palette.

None of this is uploaded anywhere. Other apps cannot read it.

Data you choose to send

Roam.Do can export a list as a .notch file so you can send it to someone else. This only ever happens when you initiate it. You choose the recipient and the app or channel used to send it, through Android's own share sheet. Roam.Do has no server and never transmits your data on its own.

Likewise, opening an item's web link or location hands the address to your browser or maps app. Once you leave Roam.Do, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

Roam.Do declares no internet permission and no location or storage permissions. The only permission it can ever ask for is camera access, and only at the moment you choose to photograph an item; you can decline and pick an existing photo instead. Photos you select through the system picker need no permission at all.

Children

Roam.Do is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete individual lists, items, or photos inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Roam.Do removes the database, all attached photos, and all preferences from your device permanently. Because nothing is stored off the device, there is nothing else to request or erase.
